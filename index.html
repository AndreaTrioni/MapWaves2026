Map Waves 2026

What is this about

Map Waves 2026 is a visual project mapping the geographical journeys and musical connections behind Waves Vienna 2026 lineup.

Diversity is one of Waves's foundational values, usually described in numbers: countries represented, nationalities on the program. Rarely shown is the shape underneath: where these artists actually come from, how far they've travelled, and the different paths that had to exist before they could all stand in the same city, in the same week.

Here each artist or band becomes one network: where each member grew up, where their paths first crossed, the stage they shared for the first time, the place a collaboration began. Laid over a map of the world, these separate networks all bend toward the same space-time coordinates: Vienna, 1-3 October 2026.
