Map Waves 2026

What is this about

Map Waves 2026 is a visual project mapping the geographical and musical journeys behind the Waves Vienna 2026 lineup.

Diversity is one of Waves's foundational values, usually described in numbers, as countries represented and nationalities on the program. But a nationality says little about who an artist really is. The real diversity lies in the paths that had to exist before everyone could stand in the same city, in the same week. Those paths reveal identity, and where they cross they show another way of belonging: not by country, but through shared routes and common events. This map follows them to Vienna.

Here each artist or band becomes a network of places: where members first met, where the band was formed, the stage they shared for the first time and the moments that marked their careers. All of these places were chosen by the artists themselves, in their own words. Laid over a map of the world, the networks bend toward the same space-time coordinates: Vienna, 1-3 October 2026.

Of the 110 artists on the lineup, 25 answered our survey and shared their route. The others appear as hollow rings with a dashed line, a placeholder marking where their journey begins.

How to explore

  • A white dot is where a band's journey begins. Tap it to read the details and see the network it belongs to. Tap it again to hide it.
  • A hollow ring is a band that hasn't shared its route yet. Its dashed line is only a placeholder.
  • The orange dot is Vienna, where every path ends.
  • Layers (top bar): switch on to keep several networks open at once; each tap adds or removes one.
  • Bands (top bar): search for a band, and choose which ones are on the map.
  • Routes (top bar): read the members' and the band's routes as a list, for every band that has shared its route.
  • Globe / flat map (top bar): switch between the two views.
  • Full screen (bottom right, below the zoom buttons, where your browser allows it).
  • × (bottom right, appears when a network is open): hide all open networks.
  • Reset (bottom right): centre the map and return to the starting zoom.
  • + / − (bottom right): zoom in and out. You can also pinch.
  • Drag the map to move around it.

Tamara Flores
Route
  • Vienna, Austria — I grew up in Vienna, where classical music and the flute have been part of my life since I was a child.
  • Musikverein, Vienna, Austria — For me, Vienna is deeply connected to its classical music tradition. One of the most formative moments of my childhood was singing Carmina Burana with my school choir at the Musikverein. I remember standing in one of those incredible halls, surrounded by a full orchestra, and feeling the sheer power of the music physically in the room. Experiencing that as a child made me understand what music can do when so many people come together to create something so big. I think that feeling has stayed with me ever since even now.
  • Wiener Konzerthaus, Vienna, Austria — I think I was about eight years old when I first experienced that kind of moment (my first-ever public show) at the Wiener Konzerthaus. Later, around 2019, I finally overcame my fear of singing in public. I went to a karaoke bar and, for the first time, performed one of my own songs, accompanying myself on the ukulele. That was a small but important turning point for me it gave me the confidence to start singing and eventually take my music to the streets of Buenos Aires.
  • Buenos Aires, Argentina — In 2019, I went to Buenos Aires for an Erasmus semester, and that's where music started to take a completely different direction for me. I began busking on the streets, improvising, and writing my own songs.
  • Vienna, Austria — Four years later, Tamara Flores emerged as my solo project bringing together all these different influences, my latin american roots but also my classical backround aaaand dance as well.
