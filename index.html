Map Waves 2026

What is this about

Map Waves 2026 is a visual project mapping the geographical journeys and musical connections behind Waves Vienna 2026 lineup.

Diversity is one of Waves's foundational values, usually described in numbers: countries represented, nationalities on the program. Rarely shown is the shape underneath: where these artists actually come from, how far they've travelled, and the different paths that had to exist before they could all stand in the same city, in the same week.

Here each artist or band becomes one network: where each member grew up, where their paths first crossed, the stage they shared for the first time, the place a collaboration began. Laid over a map of the world, these separate networks all bend toward the same space-time coordinates: Vienna, 1-3 October 2026.

How to explore

  • A white dot is where a band's journey begins. Tap it to read the details and see the network it belongs to. Tap it again to hide it.
  • A hollow ring is a band that hasn't shared its route yet. Its dashed line is only a placeholder.
  • The orange dot is Vienna, where every path ends.
  • Layers (top bar): switch on to keep several networks open at once; each tap adds or removes one.
  • Bands (top bar): search for a band, and choose which ones are on the map.
  • Globe / flat map (top bar): switch between the two views.
  • Full screen (top bar, where your browser allows it).
  • × (bottom right, appears when a network is open): hide all open networks.
  • Reset (bottom right): centre the map and return to the starting zoom.
  • + / − (bottom right): zoom in and out. You can also pinch.
  • Drag the map to move around it.
